the repository with the scripts and logs. ``Orders with $\le N$ groups'' means that orders whose SmallGroups
count exceeds $N$ were skipped; 512, 1024 and 1536 are always skipped unless stated. The table is the final version

\normalsize

Two of these deserve a comment. In 21.26 the sweep also recorded, for every group, that all inclusion-minimal
intersections $P\cap P^x$ equal $O_p(G)$---an instance of Zenkov's theorem~\cite{Zenkov} that the
agent used as a sanity check of the code. In 20.71 the computation is a nauty-based enumeration with a C filter and is the largest single
enumeration of the run; the case $k=4$ (already solved in the literature) served as a positive control, since the
filter finds examples on 9 vertices for $k=4$.
