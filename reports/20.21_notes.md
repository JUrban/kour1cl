# Problem 20.21 (G. Verret / M. Conder) — partial results

**Problem.** Is there a finite group G with normal subgroups K ≅ L, both of index 12, such that G/K ≅ C₁₂ and
G/L ≅ A₄?

**Status.** Not settled. We prove structural restrictions and rule out all groups of order ≤ 2000 as well as
all "local" configurations with |K| ≤ 252 (search over 256 in progress).

## Structure of a hypothetical example
Let I := K ∩ L. Then G/I embeds in G/K × G/L = C₁₂ × A₄ as a subdirect product, and |K/I| = |L/I| (as
|K| = |L|), K/I ≅ KL/L ⊴ A₄ and L/I ≅ KL/K ≤ C₁₂ (cyclic). Hence |K/I| = |L/I| ∈ {4, 12}, K ≠ L.

*Case |K/I| = 12* (so G/I ≅ C₁₂ × A₄, K/I ≅ A₄, L/I ≅ C₁₂): let φ: L → K be an isomorphism and I′ := φ(I).
Then K has normal subgroups I ≅ I′ with K/I ≅ A₄ and K/I′ ≅ C₁₂ — the same configuration with |K| < |G|. By
descent this case is impossible (a configuration of minimal order would have I = 1 and A₄ ≅ C₁₂).

*Case |K/I| = 4*: K/I ≅ V₄ = A₄′, L/I ≅ C₄, and G/I ≅ C₁₂ ×_{C₃} A₄ ≅ C₄ × A₄ with L/I = Z(G/I) and
K/I = (G/I)′. Any element s ∈ G mapping to a 3-cycle of A₄ acts on K/I transitively on the three non-trivial
cosets and centralises L/I. Consequently the three cosets aI, bI, abI of I in K have identical element-order
statistics, and N_m(K) − N_m(I) = 3N_m(aI), N_m(L) − N_m(I) = 2N_m(uI) + N_m(u²I) (u a generator of L/I).
Moreover X := KL satisfies X/L ≅ V₄, X/K ≅ C₄ with K ≅ L, and the isomorphism K ≅ L forces a descending chain of
pairs of isomorphic normal subgroups with quotients V₄ and C₄ (the "Q-configuration"); the chain can only
terminate in a configuration Y ⊵ A, B with Y/(A∩B) ≅ C₄ × C₂, A/(A∩B) the Frattini involution and B/(A∩B) a
non-Frattini involution of Y/(A∩B). In particular a necessary "local" condition is:

  (L) there is a group M (≅ K ≅ L) with normal subgroups I, I′ ≅ I such that M/I ≅ V₄, M/I′ ≅ C₄, and an
      automorphism of M of order 3 stabilising I and acting transitively on M/I ∖ {1}.

Abelian M never satisfy (L) (the cosets of I in M would contain elements of different orders); nor do
M = C₄ × C₂, Q₈ × C₂, Q₈ × C₄, C₄ × C₄ (direct checks).

## Computations (work/p20_21)
* `search.g`: no G of order ≤ 2000 (all orders divisible by 12, except 1536 and orders with > 30000 groups)
  has normal subgroups K ≅ L of index 12 with G/K ≅ C₁₂ and G/L ≅ A₄.

Hence an example, if it exists, has |K| = |L| ≥ 256 (probably ≥ 512) and |G| ≥ 3072.
