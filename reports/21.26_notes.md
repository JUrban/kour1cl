# Problem 21.26 (Lisi–Sabatini) — simultaneous minimal Sylow intersections

**Problem.** G a non-trivial finite group, p₁, …, p_k the distinct primes dividing |G|, H_i a Sylow p_i-subgroup.
Is there x ∈ G such that for all i the subgroup H_i ∩ H_i^x is inclusion-minimal in {H_i ∩ H_i^g : g ∈ G}?

**Status.** Open in general. (1) Verified computationally for all groups of order ≤ 2000 (except the orders
1024, 1280, 1536, 1792) and for many larger groups (see `verifications.md`, `work/p21_26`); in every case all
inclusion-minimal intersections equal O_p(G) (as predicted by Zenkov's theorem) and a common x is found by a few
random samples. (2) The following elementary observation settles the case of two primes.

**Proposition.** If |G| has exactly two prime divisors p, q, with Sylow subgroups P, Q (so that G = PQ = QP), then there
is x ∈ G with P ∩ P^x and Q ∩ Q^x both inclusion-minimal; so the answer to 21.26 is yes for such G.
*Proof.* For a ∈ P, P ∩ P^{ga} = (P ∩ P^g)^a, and conjugation by a permutes the family F_P := {P ∩ P^g : g ∈ G};
hence P ∩ P^g is inclusion-minimal in F_P iff P ∩ P^{ga} is. Choose g with P ∩ P^g minimal (F_P is finite) and
write g = ba with b ∈ Q, a ∈ P (possible as G = QP). Then P ∩ P^b = (P ∩ P^g)^{a⁻¹} is minimal. Likewise choose
h with Q ∩ Q^h minimal and write h = a′b′ (a′ ∈ P, b′ ∈ Q); then Q ∩ Q^{a′} = (Q ∩ Q^h)^{b′⁻¹} is minimal. Put
x := ba′. Then P ∩ P^x = P ∩ P^{ba′} = (P ∩ P^b)^{a′} is minimal, and Q ∩ Q^x = Q ∩ Q^{ba′} = Q ∩ (Q^b)^{a′} =
Q ∩ Q^{a′} is minimal. ∎

Remark. For three or more non-normal Sylow subgroups the analogous trick fails (with G = P₁P₂P₃ the "middle" factor is
not absorbed), and we have no proof; the computational evidence is described in `verifications.md`.
